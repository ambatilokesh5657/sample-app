Lord Rama is revered in Hinduism as the seventh incarnation of Lord Vishnu, the preserver of the universe. He represents righteousness, compassion, truth, courage, and the highest ideals of human conduct.

Known as Maryada Purushottama, meaning the ideal man who upholds righteousness, Shri Ram is celebrated for his unwavering commitment to duty, respect for elders, love for family, and dedication to justice.

His life and adventures are narrated in the ancient Hindu epic Ramayana, traditionally attributed to the sage Valmiki. The Ramcharitmanas by Goswami Tulsidas further popularized the devotion to Shri Ram.

His story continues to inspire millions of people to follow the path of truth, humility, devotion, and selfless service.
